01 About us

A data science company, built for online marketing.

Nova Insight is a technology studio. The public work is marketing: what a brand says, who should hear it, and whether the spend can be defended.

Clients arrive with a live campaign and a report that will not settle the argument. We turn that argument into a measurement, a test, or a forecast the team can keep. The company stays small so the people in the readout are the people who did the work.

A model earns its place when a marketer can argue with it.

Nova Insight sits beside an online marketing team. The launch, the audience, the flight of spend — a decision has gone soft, and the report is too polite to say so.
