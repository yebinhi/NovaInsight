04 — Fieldnote

The week intent shows up.

A representative study, composed to show the shape of an engagement.

The situation

Fieldnote bought travel intent by the month. Bookings told a sharper story: a few days after particular essays, then quiet. The rest of the flight was polite and expensive. The report, averaged across thirty days, called the month a success.

The work

We lined up search, email response, and on-site paths, and marked the weeks when intent was real for each route. Budget moved into those windows. Routes with no window were paused instead of being tuned into a smaller loss.

The month is a reporting habit. The week is when someone decides.

Creative stayed in the plan only when it showed up again in the booking week. A beautiful essay that never met a decision left the flight.

What remained

Visits converted to bookings 19% more often, on the same media budget. The calendar did the work. The team stopped buying a month and started buying the week a route was actually alive.

What the team kept

  • An intent window for each route
  • A pause rule for flights with no decision week
  • A readout that refuses to average the quiet days in
