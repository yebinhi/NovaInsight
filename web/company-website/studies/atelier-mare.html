01 — Atelier Mare

The launch that learned in public.

  • +38%Return on ad spend versus the prior launch
  • 11Weeks from first test to the kept plan
  • 2Stories carried forward, from eight
Client
Atelier Mare
Sector
Fashion
Span
11 weeks
Discipline
Experiment design

A representative study, composed to show the shape of an engagement.

The situation

Atelier Mare was eight weeks from a seasonal launch and spending as if every film, still, and city deserved the same chance. The team could feel a favorite. The media report treated all eight stories as equals. They asked for a way to let the launch teach them without burning the season.

The work

We separated the question into a sequence. First, which story. Then, which audience. Then, which city. Each stage ran only long enough to beat a threshold the team had agreed in advance, and anything that missed it left the plan the same week.

Stop averaging the creatives. Let one of them win.

The marketing team kept a single test log: hypothesis, spend, result, decision. A model stayed in the room only when it could change Monday’s buy.

What remained

Two stories carried the launch. Return on ad spend finished 38% above the prior season, on a media budget that did not grow. The test log stayed with the in-house team, ready for the next drop.

What the team kept

  • A sequence that tests story, then audience, then city
  • A rule for when a creative leaves the plan
  • A one-page log the brand team still runs
