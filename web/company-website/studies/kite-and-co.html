Spend that follows the shelf.

A representative study, composed to show the shape of an engagement.

The situation

Promotions were planned from last year’s calendar. Digital spend followed the promotion, including regions where the shelf was already heavy and the week was warm enough to slow that category. The ads arrived. The product sometimes did not need them.

The work

We joined store velocity, local weather, and media delivery into a weekly sense of where demand was about to move. Budget shifted toward regions where the product was ready and attention was cheap enough to matter. Where the shelf could not support a spike, the ads waited.

Spend is a supply decision in a media costume.

The marketing lead and the supply lead started reading the same Monday note. One page, two decisions: where to push, and where to hold.

What remained

Unsold promoted stock dropped 22% over the quarter. The media plan started to read like a supply plan: a region earned spend when the shelf could answer it.

What the team kept

  • A weekly picture of velocity, weather, and delivery
  • A hold rule for regions that cannot take a spike
  • A Monday note shared by marketing and supply
